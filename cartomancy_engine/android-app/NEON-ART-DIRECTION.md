# Astrerium Neon Cyberspace — production art direction

Edition 0.5.0, Digital Arcana revision. The artwork was restyled using the built-in image-generation tool; native Android typography, borders, elemental seals, launcher and splash marks are code-native vectors. Consumed artwork is in app/src/main/assets/astrerium, with original indexed display bounds. Moon, Temperance and Chariot remain individually packaged; image title strips are excluded from the display rectangle because titles remain upright native text.

## Digital Arcana revision

Artwork retains the established symbolic compositions while replacing painted texture with anime-influenced digital surfaces, data fragments and dark dimensional space. Violet `#8E00FF` leads, aquamarine `#00FFDA` remains, and `#6A51FF` and `#19C4FF` are tertiary accents against `#000B2B` shadow. These hex values are art-direction targets for generated imagery; native interface colors use exact values. Historical prompts and palette passes below describe prior editions, not the current raster files.

The World uses a dedicated corrected image: winged human/angel upper left, eagle upper right, bull lower left, lion lower right, matching the positions in Pamela Colman Smith's 1911 illustration for Waite's Pictorial Key. Its lower margin leaves room for the app's native title overlay.

## Palette revision 1

Palette revision 2 reverses the balance per card: violet `#8e00ff` is the principal color and aquamarine `#00ffda` the secondary accent. The established art is remapped within the indexed card bounds, preserving pixel luminance and chroma, symbolic forms and original crops. The surrounding environment remains as in revision 1.

Palette revision 3 adds tertiary blue-violet `#6A51FF` within selected violet regions of each card. Aquamarine pixels are untouched; violet remains the main color. Values and soft transitions maintain the existing painted texture.

The complete card and environment image set retains its local luminance and painterly texture while remapping saturated blue/cyan light to `#00FFDA` and saturated violet/magenta light to `#8e00ff`. Warm paint, neutral stone, skin tones, and near-black shadows remain untouched. The exact post-pass hashes and pixel counts are recorded in `astrerium/index.json`.
