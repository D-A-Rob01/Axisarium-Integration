# Spread Grammar — 0.3.4

Choose a formation for the operation you want to perform. Each new formation uses seven positions; selection remains manual. No automated recommendation or scoring is introduced.

The Constellation remains unchanged for broad systemic inquiry. Three Card remains the compact default.

## The Fork

Decision / branching.

1. **Current vector** — What direction are my present choices already taking?
2. **Path A affordance** — What might Path A make possible? Name this option before drawing.
3. **Path A cost** — What tradeoff or demand might Path A involve?
4. **Path B affordance** — What might Path B make possible? Name this option before drawing.
5. **Path B cost** — What tradeoff or demand might Path B involve?
6. **Irreversible factor** — What may be difficult to undo, and what real-world information would clarify it?
7. **Steering criterion** — What value or practical criterion could guide my choice without deciding it for me?

## The Aperture

Inquiry / reframing.

1. **Surface appearance** — What seems apparent in the situation as I currently see it?
2. **Assumption** — What am I taking for granted?
3. **Missing information** — What do I need to learn through observation or inquiry rather than infer from a card?
4. **Distortion** — What bias, fear, or framing might be shaping my account?
5. **Alternate frame** — What different perspective could I test?
6. **What becomes visible** — What possibility comes into view through that perspective?
7. **Next inquiry** — What concrete question or observation would help distinguish these possibilities?

## The Crucible

Transformation / development.

1. **Material entering** — What experiences, commitments, or resources am I bringing into this transition?
2. **Heat/pressure** — What demands are shaping the process, and which can be reduced?
3. **What resists** — What resists change, and might that resistance protect something valuable?
4. **What is being shed** — What pattern might I choose to release without assuming loss is required?
5. **What survives** — What value or capacity deserves protection through change?
6. **New property** — What capacity might emerge, without treating difficulty as a guarantee of growth?
7. **Tempering action** — What manageable action or support could help me integrate change safely?

## The Interface

Relationship / exchange.

1. **Self-state** — What am I bringing to this interaction?
2. **Other/system state** — What is my working model of the other person or system, and what evidence could check it?
3. **Contact surface** — Where does actual contact or exchange occur?
4. **Misreading/noise** — What ambiguity or assumption might interfere with understanding?
5. **Mutual affordance** — What could this interaction make possible for both sides, subject to confirmation?
6. **Boundary** — What limit, consent, or responsibility needs to remain clear?
7. **Viable exchange** — What concrete communication or exchange could I propose without presuming agreement?

## The Vector

Conditional short-range forecast.

1. **Initial condition** — What observable starting conditions matter within the time window I named?
2. **Momentum** — What pattern appears to be continuing?
3. **Accelerant** — What might strengthen or speed up that pattern?
4. **Drag** — What might slow or constrain it?
5. **Perturbation** — What change or unknown could alter the course?
6. **Steering surface** — What part of this situation can I realistically influence?
7. **Probable trajectory** — If present conditions continue, what tentative scenario could I check later? This is not a measured probability.

## Practice boundaries

Name Path A and Path B before using The Fork. Name a short time window before The Vector; its trajectory is a conditional hypothesis, never a calibrated probability. The Interface represents a working model, not privileged access to another mind. The Crucible does not imply suffering is necessary or beneficial. Missing information in The Aperture calls for real inquiry. Keep observation, association, intuition, interpretation, prediction, and recommendation distinct, and revisit the reading against what actually happened.
